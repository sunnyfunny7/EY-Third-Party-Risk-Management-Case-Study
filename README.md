# Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM) | EY Trajectory Program

## Project Overview

A cybersecurity consulting case study developed through the EY Trajectory Program, focusing on Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM), vendor risk assessment, and strategic implementation planning.

The project explored how organizations can identify, evaluate, and mitigate cybersecurity risks associated with external vendors while balancing security, cost, and operational efficiency.

## My Role

Student Consultant Intern | EY Trajectory Program

- Conducted cost-benefit analysis to evaluate TPRM implementation approaches.
- Developed vendor assessment strategies and security questionnaires.
- Designed phased implementation plans to support scalable third-party risk management.
- Analyzed cybersecurity risks associated with external vendors.
- Translated risk assessment findings into actionable stakeholder recommendations.

## Key Project Components

### 1. Vendor Risk Assessment
Evaluating third-party vendors based on cybersecurity controls, data sensitivity, business criticality, and compliance considerations.

### 2. Risk Assessment Framework
Developing a structured approach to identifying, categorizing, and prioritizing vendor risks.

### 3. Cost-Benefit Analysis
Comparing implementation strategies based on financial feasibility, operational impact, and cybersecurity benefits.

### 4. Implementation Strategy
Developing a phased approach to vendor onboarding, risk evaluation, remediation, and continuous monitoring.
